# Gini Loh — brand vocabulary

Preferred terms for this front. Enforcement lives in one place: `npm run copy:check` runs
`scripts/vibe/vibe-lint.mjs` against `scripts/vibe/lexicon.json` (vendored from the vibe-marketing
spec) on every build, and a hard violation fails it. The banned list is not restated here — a second
copy of it is how the two drift apart.

## Core architecture

- **Interactive Asset** — for "lead magnet", "whitepaper", "downloadable guide", "ebook".
- **Net Yield / ROI Engine** — positioning copy only. The search-facing word stays "calculator":
  `/calculators/…`, H1s, nav labels and slugs are never renamed.
- **Agentic Plumbing** — for "backend automation", "workflow scripts", "cron jobs".
- **Precision Mechanics** — for "advanced features", "sophisticated tools", "bells and whistles".
- **Decision Engine** — for "blog post", "article", "financial advice piece".

## Domain vocabulary (Gini Loh verticals)

- **Cliff Forfeiture** — the unvested equity liability incurred when departing before a vesting cliff.
- **Single-Stock Concentration Liability** — holding employer equity alongside wage income.
- **Loaded Engineering Rate** — true internal engineering cost, $185–$250/hr with benefits, payroll
  taxes, tooling and equity overhead.
- **Maintenance Multiplier** — the permanent fractional headcount needed to keep internal tooling
  from decaying.
- **Utilization Threshold** — the break-even hours per month where owned hardware beats rented cloud.
- **Exit Liability** — the penalty schedule for terminating a residential or commercial lease.
- **Frictionless Capital Sweep** — rule-based liquidity allocation between operating cash and
  high-yield vehicles.

## Structure

Start with the metric, the problem statement, or the conclusion. No introductory paragraph.
