# Vibe Marketing Lexicon for Gini Loh

## Preferred Terminology (High-Signal Technical Vocabulary)

### Core Architecture
- **Interactive Asset**: Replaces "lead magnet", "whitepaper", "downloadable guide", or "ebook".
- **Net Yield / ROI Engine**: Replaces "calculator", "estimator", or "survey" when financial or capital metrics are involved.
- **Agentic Plumbing**: Replaces "backend automation", "workflow scripts", or "cron jobs".
- **Precision Mechanics**: Replaces "advanced features", "sophisticated tools", or "bells and whistles".
- **Decision Engine**: Replaces "blog post", "article", or "financial advice piece".

### Domain-Specific Vocabulary (Gini Loh Verticals)
- **Cliff Forfeiture**: The unvested equity liability incurred when departing before an equity vesting cliff.
- **Single-Stock Concentration Liability**: The double jeopardy of holding employer equity alongside wage income.
- **Loaded Engineering Rate**: True internal engineering cost including benefits, payroll taxes, tooling, and equity overhead ($185–$250/hr).
- **Maintenance Multiplier**: The permanent ongoing fractional headcount required to keep internal tooling from decaying.
- **Utilization Threshold**: The break-even hours per month where owned hardware outperforms rented cloud instances.
- **Exit Liability**: The legally enforceable penalty schedule for terminating residential or commercial lease agreements.
- **Frictionless Capital Sweep**: Automated rule-based liquidity allocation between operational cash and high-yield vehicles.

## Prohibited Terms (Forbidden AI-isms & Corporate Filler)

### Banned Buzzwords
- **delve** (e.g., "Let's delve into the details...")
- **harness** (e.g., "Harness the power of data...")
- **testament** (e.g., "A testament to its performance...")
- **tapestry** (e.g., "A rich tapestry of solutions...")
- **landscape** (e.g., "In the evolving financial landscape...")
- **beacon** (e.g., "A beacon of hope for startups...")
- **unleash** (e.g., "Unleash your productivity...")
- **supercharge** (e.g., "Supercharge your workflow...")
- **game-changer** (e.g., "This tool is a real game-changer...")
- **seamless** (e.g., "Enjoy seamless integration...")
- **revolutionize** (e.g., "Revolutionizing the way engineers decide...")
- **holistic / synergy / paradigm shift / robust** (Avoid generic corporate puffery)

### Prohibited Introductory Phrases (Throat-Clearing)
- *"In today's fast-paced digital world..."*
- *"In the modern enterprise landscape..."*
- *"It is important to remember that..."*
- *"Without further ado, let's explore..."*
- *"Whether you are a seasoned veteran or just starting out..."*
- *"At the end of the day, it all boils down to..."*

*Rule: Start directly with the metric, the problem statement, or the quantitative conclusion.*
