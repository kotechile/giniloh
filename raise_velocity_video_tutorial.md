# Video Script & Tutorial: The Raise Velocity Calculator

This guide provides a step-by-step video outline, storyboard, and voiceover script for demonstrating the **Raise Velocity Calculator** on Giniloh. It is structured to maintain high viewer retention and explain the math clearly.

---

## 🎥 Video Details
* **Target Length**: 3 - 4 minutes (YouTube / LinkedIn) or 60 seconds (Shorts/Reels split)
* **Visual Style**: Clean screenshares of the calculator UI, mixed with zoom-ins on interactive charts and data highlights.
* **Goal**: Show professionals how to calculate their true "Loyalty Tax" versus "Job-Hopping Friction".

---

## 🎬 Storyboard & Script

### Hook (0:00 - 0:25)
* **Visual**: Show the user's cursor hovering over the **compounding chart** showing the gap widening over 10 years, then zoom in on the **$11,576 delta** metric.
* **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Did you know switching jobs isn't always as profitable as it looks on paper? It’s called the 'Job-Hopping Tax.' While you might get a higher base salary, unvested 401(k) matches, bonus clawbacks, and health insurance gaps can destroy your gains. Today, we're using Giniloh's Raise Velocity Calculator to find your mathematical sweet spot."*

---

### Step 1: Set the Baseline (0:25 - 0:55)
* **Visual**: Click on the **⚙️ Core parameters** tab. Clear the starting salary box and type in `100000` (highlighting the newly updated alignment!). Show the tax rate slider set to `30%`.
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"First, let's open the Core Parameters. We'll start with a baseline salary of $100,000. Next, enter your combined marginal tax rate—let's keep it at 30%. We'll also assume you invest your raise differences in a brokerage account compounding at an average annual return of 8%."*

---

### Step 2: Define the "Stayer" Path (0:55 - 1:20)
* **Visual**: Switch to the **🏠 Stayer settings** tab. Highlight the **Annual Merit Raise** box (set to `4.1%`).
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"Next, click on Stayer Settings. This represents staying at your current company. According to national averages, internal merit raises hover around 4.1%. We'll keep our expected weekly hours at 40 and PTO at 15 days to establish our baseline hourly rate."*

---

### Step 3: Model the "Switcher" Strategy (1:20 - 1:55)
* **Visual**: Click on the **🚗 Switcher details** tab. Zoom in on **Hop Interval (Years)** (set to `3`) and **Hop Salary Increase** (set to `15%`).
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"Now, let's look at the Switcher strategy. We'll set our hop interval to 3 years. This means you switch jobs every 3 years. When you hop, we'll model an average salary bump of 15%. In non-switch years, we'll assume a standard internal raise of 3%."*

---

### Step 4: Account for Transition Friction (1:55 - 2:30)
* **Visual**: Scroll down to **New Hire Signing Bonus** and **COBRA Transition Cost** in the Switcher panel. Highlight where these frictional costs are defined.
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"Here's where the magic happens: the Job-Hopping Tax. The calculator factors in retirement match forfeitures—if your old company has a 3-year cliff vesting schedule, you lose your unvested matches when you leave. We also add a $1,200 out-of-pocket COBRA cost to cover health insurance gaps, offset by a $5,000 signing bonus."*

---

### Step 5: Read the Chart & Analyze the Results (2:30 - 3:15)
* **Visual**: Toggle the chart from **Salary trajectory** (showing Switcher salary climb to **$167,756** vs Stayer salary of **$143,568**) to **Portfolio growth** (showing the final Year 10 compounded value of **$11,576**).
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"Now look at the charts. On the Salary Trajectory view, the Switcher's base salary climbs to $167,756 compared to the Stayer's $143,568 (+17% premium). But look at the Portfolio Growth view. Even with that higher salary, the Switcher only walks away with an extra $11,576 in invested wealth over 10 years. Why? Because the unvested 401(k) matches and lost bonuses ate away almost all of their gains. This proves that you must negotiate a higher signing bonus to cover those forfeited assets!"*

---

### Call to Action (3:15 - 3:30)
* **Visual**: Show the full dashboard, then zoom out to the URL: `giniloh.com/calculators/raise-velocity`.
* **Voiceover**:
  > *"Stop guessing if that recruiter's offer is worth it. Go to giniloh.com, plug in your exact numbers, and calculate your true career velocity today."*
